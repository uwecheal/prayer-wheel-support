Privacy Policy · 隐私政策

Mani Khorlo · Prayer Wheel · Effective 25 November 2026

English

This app does not collect any personal data.

  • No account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
  • Your counts, practice calendar and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Motion data (used to turn the wheel when you move your device) is processed on the device in real time and is not stored or transmitted.
  • Optional support purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment or personal details.
  • The app makes no network connections of its own.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: ponyocheal@gmail.com
