Support

Always free, no ads, no subscriptions, no account. The app works fully offline on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch.

Turning the wheel: swipe left across the wheel, tap Auto-spin, or (on iPhone and iPad) gently move the device in small circles. On Apple Watch, turn the Digital Crown.

Sound and haptics: tap the speaker button on the home screen to switch the ritual bell, wheel sound and haptics on or off.

Your counts are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Voluntary support at the bottom of Settings is an optional tip. It unlocks nothing and is handled by Apple.

Questions or feedback: ponyocheal@gmail.com

技术支持

永久免费，无广告，无订阅，无需注册，可在 iPhone、iPad、Mac 和 Apple Watch 上完全离线使用。

转经：在经筒上向左滑动，或点「自动转动」；在 iPhone 和 iPad 上也可手持设备轻轻画圈。Apple Watch 上转动表冠即可。

声音与触感：点首页的喇叭按钮，可分别开关法铃声、经筒转动声和振动。

计数只保存在您的设备上，删除应用即一并删除。

自愿支持位于「设置」底部，是可选的打赏，不解锁任何功能，由 Apple 处理付款。

问题或建议请写信至 ponyocheal@gmail.com
